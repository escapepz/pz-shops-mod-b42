[h1]Shops B42 (B42.13 Multiplayer)[/h1]

[b]Unofficial B42.13 port of the official [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2840330802]Shops mod[/url] (originally for B41)[/b]

This is an enhanced version of the popular Shops mod, updated and optimized for PZ Build 42.13.1 Multiplayer. It retains all core features from the original while adding new functionality and improvements for B42.13 MP stability.

[h2]New in B42.13 (This Port)[/h2]

[b]Search & Discovery[/b] - Real-time item search with case-insensitive filtering across all tabs

[b]Container Viewer (Bagception)[/b] - View [strike]and purchase[/strike] items nested inside bags/containers displayed in shop UIs

[strike][b]Dynamic Pricing[/b] - Drainable items adjust price based on remaining units[/strike]

[b]Offline Mailbox Transfer System[/b]

When you transfer money to a player who is [b]offline[/b]:
[list]
[*] The transfer is stored in a "BalanceMailbox" (server-side ModData)
[*] The recipient's account is marked with [i]hasMailbox = true[/i]
[*] When the recipient logs in, a "Claim Offline Mailbox" option appears in their wallet context menu
[*] Right-click wallet → "Claim Offline Mailbox" to receive all pending transfers at once
[*] The system shows a notification with total coins received and number of transfers
[*] Support for both normal and special currency types
[*] Each transfer is timestamped and tracked by actionId
[/list]

[b]Security Enhancements[/b]
[list]
[*] Transaction Registry prevents duplication/replay attacks
[*] Proximity validation (2-tile purchase range on server)
[*] [strike]Rate limiting (3 transfers per 10 seconds)[/strike]
[*] Comprehensive audit logging of all transactions (WIP)
[/list]
